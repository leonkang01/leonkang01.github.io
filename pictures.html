Gallery

A collection of different highlights and memories in my life.

Babloa Park

I went here with my family and it was really interesting, we went to the International Cottages and ate different foods along with watching a Hula Dance demonstration.

Mountain Spirit Center

I went to this Buddhist Temple and it was way in the middle of nowhere so the atmosphere was really calm and peaceful, since my family were the only ones there we got to ring this huge bell which echoed through the mountains.

The Flower Fields

My family visited the Flower Fields and we got to relax and look at a ton of different plants, the air was really clear and it looked like waves of color that didnt stop until the horizon.

Getty Museum

My family visits a lot of museums and we recently went to the Getty. In order to see the displays we had to ride a cable car up a mountain which was almost cooler than the art and furniture, almost.

Mount Diablo

I hiked Mount Diablo in SF with some family members for thanksgiving. Getting to look down and see clouds was an incredibly interesting experience and I had a great time burning off the thanksgiving calories.
